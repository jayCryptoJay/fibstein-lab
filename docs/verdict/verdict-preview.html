5 modelling assumptions behind this result
  • Liquidation is approximated from trade-price candles and a fixed maintenance rate.
  • Spread and slippage are modelled; OHLCV cannot establish book depth or queue position.
  • Entry size is capped by the previous candle’s volume.
  • Funding uses historical rates, floored to the execution boundary.
  • Drawdown uses candle closes; intra-candle drawdown can be larger.
